My background

I specialize in helping individuals heal from trauma, depression, and the lasting impact of interpersonal violence, using evidence-based approaches that are both effective and compassionate.

As a Filipino-American who has worked with people from diverse backgrounds, I deeply value the role of cultural identity in our emotional lives. I strive to create a space where all aspects of who you are, your race, gender, sexuality, or socioeconomic status, are acknowledged and respected.

I earned my PhD in Clinical Psychology from Saint Louis University and a Master of Public Health from San Francisco State University. My clinical work has included trauma-focused care in several settings, including the VA healthcare system.

Whether you’re navigating painful past experiences, daily stress, or simply feeling stuck, my goal is to provide a safe, inclusive space where you feel seen and supported as you grow.

My approach

I provide therapy to adults (18 and older) who live in California, exclusively through secure telehealth sessions. I use evidence-based cognitive behavioral therapies tailored to your needs, so you can understand the link between thoughts, emotions, and behavior and build tools that last.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

A practical, goal-oriented therapy that helps you identify and shift unhelpful thought patterns. Effective for anxiety, depression, and everyday stress.

Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT)

A structured, research-supported treatment for PTSD that looks at how trauma affects beliefs about yourself and the world.

Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) skills

Strategies for emotional regulation, distress tolerance, and relationships, especially when you feel overwhelmed or reactive.

Problem Solving Therapy (PST)

An action-oriented approach that builds skills to manage stressors and face obstacles with more clarity.

Prolonged Exposure (PE)

A highly effective PTSD treatment where you safely approach trauma-related memories and situations you have been avoiding.

Mindfulness and strengths-based practice

Staying grounded in the present and connected to your resilience as you heal and grow.

F.A.Q.

The questions clients most often ask before starting therapy.

What can I expect during our first session?

A 60-minute intake where we explore your concerns, background, and goals, and see whether we’re a good fit.

How often will we meet?

Most clients begin weekly to build momentum and consistency. We can adjust the frequency as therapy progresses.

Do you offer in-person sessions?

No. I provide therapy exclusively via telehealth to adults who live in California, on a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform through SimplePractice.

Can I use my insurance?

I’m not in-network with any insurance providers, but I can provide a superbill for possible out-of-network reimbursement.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Credit and debit cards, processed securely through SimplePractice. Payment is due at the time of each session.

Do you offer a sliding scale?

Yes, a limited number of spots for clients facing financial hardship. Bring it up in our initial consultation.

What is your cancellation policy?

I require at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations or missed appointments are charged the full session fee, except in emergencies.
